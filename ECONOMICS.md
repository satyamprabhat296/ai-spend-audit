# ECONOMICS.md

# Assumptions

This analysis uses rough startup-stage assumptions rather than enterprise-scale benchmarks. The goal is to test whether the AI Spend Audit tool could realistically become a profitable lead-generation channel for Credex.

The product itself is free. Revenue comes indirectly through converting high-intent users into Credex consultations and eventually financial products.

---

# What Is a Converted Lead Worth?

If a company completes the audit, books a consultation, and later purchases a Credex financial product, the expected annual revenue could reasonably look like:

| Revenue Source | Estimated Annual Value |
|---|---|
| Credit / financing fees | $1,500 |
| SaaS or platform revenue | $500 |
| Retained account value | $1,000 |

Estimated annual value per converted customer:

# ≈ $3,000/year

This estimate assumes:
- startup customers,
- small-to-mid-sized teams,
- recurring platform engagement,
- moderate financial product usage.

# What Must Be True To Reach $1M ARR?

Several conditions must hold:

## 1. The Audit Must Feel Immediately Valuable

Users should discover real savings quickly.
If recommendations feel generic, conversion rates collapse.

---

## 2. Trust Is Critical

The audit must appear financially credible.
Bad pricing assumptions or unrealistic recommendations would destroy conversion.

---

## 3. Distribution Must Compound

The tool works best if:
- founders share results publicly,
- agencies reuse it,
- finance teams use it repeatedly,
- SEO captures AI pricing searches.

Without distribution, CAC rises too quickly.

---

## 4. AI Tool Spend Must Continue Growing

This business thesis depends on AI software fragmentation continuing.

If companies consolidate around a single provider, optimization opportunities decrease significantly.

---

# Final Assessment

The economics appear realistic for an early-stage B2B lead-generation product.

The strongest signal is not traffic volume — it is whether users trust the audit enough to continue into a financial consultation flow.

If the audit consistently surfaces meaningful savings opportunities, the conversion funnel could support a profitable acquisition engine for Credex.
