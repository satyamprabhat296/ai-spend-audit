# Pricing Strategy Notes

The audit engine intentionally uses:
- publicly visible pricing
- normalized monthly estimates
- simplified seat-based calculations

This keeps recommendations:
- transparent
- deterministic
- easy to test

Enterprise plans with undisclosed pricing were estimated conservatively to preserve realistic audit behavior.
