# FAQ

## 1. What does the AI Spend Audit do?

The tool analyzes your current AI subscriptions, monthly spend, and team usage patterns to recommend more cost-efficient plans.

---

## 2. Which AI tools are supported?

Currently supported:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot

Additional tools can be added easily through the pricing configuration.

---

## 3. Is my pricing data stored?

No sensitive billing information is stored. The tool only uses the numbers you manually enter for analysis purposes.

---

## 4. How are savings calculated?

Savings are estimated by comparing your current plan against lower-cost alternatives that better fit your team size and use case.

---

## 5. Who is this tool built for?

The product is designed for:
- startups
- indie hackers
- agencies
- engineering teams
- AI-heavy businesses managing multiple subscriptions

# Positioning

AI subscription costs are becoming increasingly fragmented across teams. Most startups accumulate overlapping AI tools without visibility into ROI or plan efficiency.

AI Spend Audit helps companies quickly identify unnecessary enterprise plans, duplicate tooling, and optimization opportunities before costs scale further.
